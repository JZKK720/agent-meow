## Improve first

Finding #1 (out-of-order TTS playback) is the highest-leverage fix. It directly affects the user's experience of the voice feature — sentences playing out of order makes the assistant's speech incoherent. The fix is a small change to `processTurn()` (chain the `flushSentence` calls with `await` instead of `void`), and it has the most visible impact on functionality smoothness.
